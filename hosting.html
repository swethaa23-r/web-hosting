How do I choose the right hosting plan?

If you're just starting out, the Starter plan is perfect. If you expect high traffic or run an e-commerce store, we recommend the Professional or Enterprise plans for their increased RAM and CPU allocations.

Will you help me migrate my existing website?

Yes! We offer free, seamless website migrations with zero downtime. Once you purchase a plan, simply open a migration ticket and our experts will handle the rest.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Absolutely. You can easily upgrade or downgrade your hosting plan at any time directly from your control panel without experiencing any downtime.

What happens if I exceed my bandwidth limit?

Our Professional and Enterprise plans include unlimited bandwidth. On the Starter plan, we will send you a warning email if you approach your limit, giving you time to upgrade without your site going offline.
