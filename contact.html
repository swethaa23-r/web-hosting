Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common queries regarding sales, technical tickets, response times, and server migrations.

Have a question not listed here? Our support team is online 24/7/365 to assist you.

How quickly does your technical support team respond?

Our 24/7 technical support team maintains an average first-response time of under 15 minutes for support tickets, while live chat inquiries are typically answered in under 2 minutes. Critical infrastructure alerts are routed immediately to on-duty Tier-3 systems engineers.

Where can I submit a high-priority support ticket?

Active clients can log into the Stackly Client Portal and submit tickets directly under Support > Open Ticket. Selecting 'High' or 'Critical' priority routes your issue to senior technicians with expedited SLAs.

Do you provide free website and cPanel migrations?

Yes! We provide 100% free, white-glove website migrations with zero downtime. Once you order any hosting plan, simply provide your previous host's cPanel or FTP credentials and our migration specialists will handle the transfer and DNS switch.

How do I request a custom enterprise or dedicated server quote?

You can submit your custom CPU, RAM, NVMe storage, and bandwidth requirements using the contact form above, or email enterprise@stackly.com. Our cloud solution architects prepare custom quotes and architecture blueprints within 2 to 4 business hours.

What payment options and refund guarantees do you offer?

We accept all major credit/debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex), PayPal, Apple Pay, and UPI. All shared and WordPress hosting packages are backed by an unconditional 30-day money-back guarantee.
