What is NVMe SSD web hosting, and why is it faster?

NVMe (Non-Volatile Memory Express) SSDs communicate directly with your server's motherboard rather than through legacy SATA ports. This results in up to 10x faster data read/write speeds, drastically reducing page load times and database query latency.

Can I easily upgrade my hosting plan as my business grows?

Yes! You can instantly scale your hosting plan or allocate more CPU, RAM, and NVMe disk space directly from your control panel without any server downtime.
