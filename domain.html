What is a domain name?

A domain name is your website's address on the internet (like stackly.com). It's what users type into their browsers to find your website. Without a domain name, people would have to memorize your server's IP address.

Can I transfer a domain I already own?

Yes! You can transfer any eligible domain to Stackly. You'll need the EPP authorization code from your current registrar and to ensure the domain is unlocked. Our support team can assist you throughout the process.

What is Domain Privacy Protection?

When you register a domain, your contact details (name, email, phone) are added to a public database called WHOIS. Domain Privacy hides your personal information, replacing it with generic registrar data to prevent spam and identity theft.

How long does it take for a domain to become active?

Once you register a domain name, it is reserved immediately. However, it can take anywhere from a few minutes up to 48 hours for the DNS records to propagate globally across the internet.
